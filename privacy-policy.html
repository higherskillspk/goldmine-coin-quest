Goldmine Coin Quest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Goldmine Coin Quest (package com.higherskills.goldmine.coinquest), published by Higher Skills Games, uses and why.

Information the game itself stores

Your game progress (coins, levels, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers, do not ask you to create an account, and do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and app usage and interaction data. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Menu → Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can deliver your item. We never see or store your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Prize wheel odds

The Ancient Wheel shows the odds of every prize on screen before you spin.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact us.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information collected by Google is handled under Google's own policies and retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal information on our own systems, most requests concern Google; you can use the in-game Ad Privacy Choices, your device's ad settings, or Google's tools at myaccount.google.com. You can also contact us below.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
